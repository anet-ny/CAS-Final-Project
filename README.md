# CAS-Final-Project
## chat-room.ch 2.0 (working title)
### Abstract
chat-room.ch 2.0 is a multimodal chatroom in which an AI moderator and interpreter ena-bles people, with or without impairments, to participate in a shared conversational space using the input modalities they choose (speech, text, gesture, or sensor-based signals). The piece explores the role of the AI moderator and interpreter and how it differs from the role AI has in applications such as WhatsApp and WeChat.
chat-room.ch 2.0 tests "doing space" on a web-based IMP, reclaiming the chatroom as moderately structured, sensor-rich architecture where diverse bodies co-produce conversational space.

“I’m taking the methods from barrier-free translation practice (STTI, respeaking, audio de-scription, accessible web design) and re-situating them in a creative, sensor-driven conver-sational environment, where smartphones serve as inclusive sensing devices rather than just screens.” Anet Nyffeler
Calculations were performed on UBELIX (https://www.id.unibe.ch/hpc), the HPC cluster at the University of Bern
